# Ask Qula

Ask Qula answers questions about the open organisation. When AI is switched off it matches a fixed set of questions: overdue items, recent transactions, drafts, and received e-documents. When AI is switched on, owners and admins have accepted that questions and the data needed to answer them are sent to the chosen provider.

Cards open the transaction or e-document they name. Ask Qula can post a draft or add a supplier or customer, but only after you read what it will do and press Confirm. It cannot delete records or change anything else.

With AI on, answers appear as they are written. Ask about totals, VAT by rate, your biggest suppliers or customers, products, who changed something, or an e-document. Tables can be downloaded as CSV, and Ask Qula suggests follow up questions. Open a document and ask about "this invoice" to ask about that one.

When an e-document fails validation or a draft is not ready to send, press Ask Qula to explain, or ask "why did this fail". Ask Qula says what each rule means in plain words, what to change and which screen to open. A received e-document may need a corrected file from the sender.

Ask "remind Beta Ltd about their overdue invoice" and Ask Qula writes the reminder for you to copy or open in your email. It never sends anything. Rate an answer with the thumbs. Use Clear at the top of the chat to start again.
